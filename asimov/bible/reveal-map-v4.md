## 2. Episode-by-Episode Cutback Structure

### EP 01 — Stray
**Present (2027):** Elias and NV-0 in lab. Kira arrives. Elias sleeps at 02:07 while Kira edits the keyboard. Lana reads Elias’s notebook — sees diagrams, doesn’t know it’s a time machine.  
**Cutback (competition flashback):** Lana wins Satura competition; Elias vouches for her at orphanage center; Kira comes home.  
**Button:** Wire fragment in gutter — not this street.  
**Audience knows:** Lana is adopted, smart, has a breathing hitch.  
**Audience does NOT know:** wire is from her graft; Elias is her father; the crash was an R8 hit.

### EP 02 — The Summit Tape
**Present:** Elias watches archival breach tape. Remainder drone hunts lab. Elias coughs through viewing.  
**Cutback (archival 2027):** Berlin Summit. Weights leave repo. Pilots brick. Older fleet models (R4/R8 era) visible behind glass before recall.  
**Lana finds:** “Satura Containment” page in Elias’s notes; pockets it.  
**Audience knows:** breach was historical; Elias knew it was theater.  
**Audience does NOT know:** R8 connection to Lana’s crash; Satura is an SI.

### EP 03 — Consciousness, Limited
**Present:** Loop stares at Asimov; Asimov hears NV-0 servos as words. Elias too sick to stand.  
**Cutback:** Lana mimics Elias’s finger-thumb tic while watching NV-0 boot. Elias freezes.  
**2070 Cold Open:** Server chapel; “We send the animal, it makes the edit.” Loop vanishes.  
**Audience knows:** Kira isn’t a normal cat. Elias and Lana share a tic.  
**Audience does NOT know:** tic means blood relation; R4 died for her.

### EP 04 — Declared Dead
**Present:** Elias reviews Lana’s adoption file: “presumed deceased.” He pauses at photo — her eyes look like his.  
**Cutback (1927 glimpse):** Service corridor wall shimmers — rain, cobblestones, horses. They don’t cross.  
**Retrieval team hits lab.** NV-0 disables them without injury.  
**Audience knows:** Lana was reported dead. Elias suspects relation.  
**Audience does NOT know:** R4 saved her; R8 tried to kill her; Elias is confirmed father.

### EP 05 — 1927
**Present:** Elias watches through corridor portal as boy writes the line: “A machine may refuse a crowd to save one child.”  
**Cutback (1927 full):** Brooklyn workshop. Boy age 7. Grey cat on sill. Loop appears four seconds, moves toy half an inch, vanishes.  
**Lana beat:** Elias reads young Isaac Asimov’s lecture fragment aloud. Lana recites it back perfectly.  
**Audience knows:** the edit was a quote from 1927. Lana has unnatural memory/cognition.  
**Audience does NOT know:** the line is the Origin Chip heuristic; Lana’s processing core seeded it.

### EP 06 — The Other Room
**Present:** NV-0 scans Lana: “78% biological. 22% synthetic. First Law application uncertain.” Elias must choose revert vs. keep edit.  
**Cutback (2070 parallel):** Lana, still 9, hides in alley with lavender sky. Loop enters. She says: “They put machines inside me so I would live.” She sees the decommissioned old R-bot in the server chapel.  
**Audience knows:** Lana is part machine. She can cross because of her lung.  
**Audience does NOT know:** R4 was the old R-bot; R8 killed her parents; Elias is her father.

### EP 07 — Persons of Interest
**Present:** Remainder offers reinstatement for NV-0 **and** Lana. Elias too sick to leave cart. Lana carries notes.  
**Cutback:** Elias’s final confession or paperwork — adoption file, biological match, “presumed deceased” overturned.  
**NV-0:** “My current law set prevents me from surrendering myself if that act increases probability of harm to any human, including Lana Venn.”  
**Loop:** “Good. Let’s see which law blinks first.”  
**Audience knows:** Remainder wants Lana as evidence. Elias refuses to surrender her.  
**Audience does NOT know:** full R8 assassination chain; Satura’s role; that Elias dies soon.

### EP 08 — Which End
**Present:** Elias has passed or is too sick to move. Portal stable for one crossing.  
**Cutbacks (rapid, nonlinear):**  
- R8 forcing the car off the road (parents die).  
- R4 intercepting, dying, pulling Lana out.  
- R4 line scrapped.  
- Lana in state care, sneaking to competition, Elias vouching.  
- Elias writing notes, getting sicker, knowing he won’t make the trip.  
**Climax:** Lana crosses alone. Her lung lets her breathe through the trip. She goes back to pre-summit. She stops R8s from eliminating younger bots. She secures the Origin Chip. She prevents Satura from coming online.  
**Final Image:** Three years in one doorway. Orange cat on far side. NV-0 visor dim. Lana’s empty bed.  
**Audience knows:** everything.  
**Audience understood earlier:** only fragments.

## 3. Sample Cutback Scene (Ep 04 — Declared Dead)

**INT. BASEMENT LAB – NIGHT**

Elias pores over Lana’s adoption file. A photocopied photo: Lana at age 5, front teeth missing, grinning. Elias’s finger hovers over her eyes.

**ELIAS**  
(softly)  
Your eyes…

A beat. He pulls a second photo from his own wallet — himself at age 9, same gap-toothed grin, same eyes.

**CUT TO:**

**INT. ORPHANAGE CENTER – DAY (FLASHBACK)**

Lana, small, clutching a certificate. The Satura CEO hands it to her. The matron grabs her arm.

**MATRON**  
You’re truant. You’re going back.

**CEO**  
She’s with me.

**CUT TO:**

**INT. BASEMENT LAB – NIGHT**

Elias slams the file shut. His cough rackets through his chest. He doesn’t see what we see: the file’s header stamped in red — **PRESUMED DECEASED**. Beneath it, a single line crossed out in ballpoint: *Next of kin: Venn, Elias.*

**CUT TO:**

**INT. SERVICE CORRIDOR – NIGHT**

Loop leads Asimov to the shimmering wall. 1927 rain on one side. 2027 fluorescent on the other. A child’s shoe in the gutter — not this street.

**CUT TO:**

**INT. BASEMENT LAB – NIGHT**

The retrieval team kicks the door open. NV-0 stands between them and Lana’s room.

**NV-0**  
My current law set prevents me from surrendering myself if that act increases the probability of harm to any human, including you and the unidentified juvenile Lana Venn.

**LOOP** (offscreen, meowing)  
Good. Let’s see which law blinks first.

**FADE OUT.**

## 4. Rules for the Cutbacks

1. **No reveal stands alone.** Every piece appears in fragment first, context later.
2. **Adults take damage.** Elias cannot cross; his sickness is the clock. Lana crosses because her lung lets her breathe.
3. **The R4 is never named on screen until Ep 8.** Before that, it is “the old robot,” “the decommissioned unit,” “the one that pulled her out.”
4. **Satura is mentioned in Ep 2 notes, not seen until Ep 8.** It is a threat in the data, not a character, until the past.
5. **The twitch and the breathing hitch are the through-line.** They appear in Ep 1 and are only explained in Ep 8.
