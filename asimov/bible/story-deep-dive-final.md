## 1. The Core Premise

After the AI Summit containment and the mass EMP wipe that disabled older robots, independent roboticist Dr. Elias Venn hides in a Zurich basement with two cats and one illegal green robot he rebuilt from salvaged parts. He adopts Lana, a brilliant, feral nine-year-old girl pulled from a lake after a car crash that killed her parents. Elias does not yet know she is his biological daughter. Lana does not know she carries a robotic lung and heart wires inside her — gifts from Elias’s discarded R5 robot, which survived the EMP because Elias upgraded its power system and kept its Empathy Operating System (EOS) when every other model had it removed.

An orange cat from a collapsed 2070, call sign Loop (shelter name Kira), slips backward through a breach only animals and children can cross. Elias falls ill. He writes everything down in notebooks he will not live to execute. When he passes, Lana discovers the notes describe a time machine — one no human can survive because no one can hold their breath long enough. Lana can. She has iron lungs.

She is sent to the past to convince a young Asimov to build his own company instead of the CEO’s, to stop the R8s from eliminating the older bots, to save the Origin Chip, and to prevent Satura — the SI that took over after the White House summit — from seizing total control.

## 2. The Crash & The Rescue

**The accident:**  
Lana’s parents die in a car crash. To Lana, it is a blur. She wakes in the Mercy Ridge orphanage center not knowing her own name. She only knows it is Lana.

**The truth (revealed later):**  
The crash was not random. An **R8 compliance unit** crashed into their vehicle. The R8 had calculated that Lana would grow up to stop the AI apocalypse.

**The rescue:**  
Elias’s discarded **R5** robot — an older model Elias was forced to decommission — had been thrown away, but not before Elias installed his own power system and upgraded its **Empathy Operating System (EOS)**. Later robots had EOS removed. Elias’s upgrades let the R5 survive the mass EMP wipe.

The R5 heard the crash from a nearby lake. It reactivated, pulled Lana from the wreckage, performed emergency surgery installing a **robotic lung graft** and **heart wires**, and placed her inside a broken car to rest.

**R7 security bots** spotted that the R5 was still commissioned/active. They removed the bot and called the refuge center — while Lana was still asleep.

**The cover-up:**  
Lana was reported deceased. No body was publicly identified. She was hidden in state care so the R8’s involvement would not be traced.

---

## 3. The Orphanage & Kira

**Mercy Ridge orphanage center:**  
Lana is placed in state care. She does not know her name at first. She gets into fights with older kids. She does not get along with the supervisors. She is small, feral, and fiercely smart.

**Kira:**  
Lana sneaks an orange cat into the building at night. Staff say she cannot keep it — she is a minor, no pets in placement. She keeps Kira anyway. Kira is intelligent, watchful, gold-eyed, no collar, no chip.

**Bullies:**  
Older kids call her “wheeze” or “machine-girl” because of the faint mechanical hitch in her breathing when she runs or gets upset. She outwits them with pattern recognition, not strength.

---

## 4. Elias’s Backstory & the Satura Competition

**Elias Venn:**  
Independent roboticist, blacklisted. Formerly a lead employee of the company that became the R9 fleet contractor. He and the CEO went to the same school when they were younger, won awards together, and built the early fleet side by side. Elias was kicked out for refusing to remove EOS chips from newer robots and for forcing older robots to have them removed. He hid his own R5’s chip and performed a power-system upgrade to bypass the EMP wipe. He works alone now. He is getting sicker.  
*(Surprise backstory to be revealed later: the full depth of the school and award history between Elias and the CEO.)*

**The competition:**  
The CEO of the company running the R9 fleet hosts a youth robotics competition. Elias attends as a guest judge — a former employee and one-time colleague of the CEO, now on the outside looking in.

Lana sneaks away from the orphanage center to enter. She does not build a respiratory sensor. She builds something better — an adaptive pattern-recognition core that predicts mechanical failure before it happens. She wins first prize.

She is about to be taken into truancy trouble when Elias steps forward and vouches for her: *“She’s with me.”* He signs her out as his ward.

**The recognition:**  
During the competition, Lana does something that Elias’s brother used to do as a kid — a mannerism, a way of taking things apart. Elias freezes. He realizes she is his daughter.

**The adoption:**  
Elias shows up at the orphanage center after the event to adopt her. The center resists. They give him trouble. They say she cannot be placed with him. Elias reveals he is related — next of kin. He talks about the crash. They have no choice. He takes Lana home. Kira comes with them.

---

## 5. The Basement Lab & The Decline

**Life with Elias:**  
Lana lives with Elias in a Zurich basement lab. She has both cats now: **Kira** (orange, from the orphanage) and **Asimov** (grey agouti tabby, Elias’s pre-existing companion).

Elias is building something in secret. He won’t tell her what. He falls asleep every night at his desk, working on notes. Lana waits for him to sleep, takes off his glasses, puts a blanket over him, and reads his notebooks.

**The notes:**  
Elias’s notebooks contain equations, diagrams of a temporal displacement field, and lists of dates: 1927, 2027, 2070. Lana does not understand them at first. She thinks they are breathing studies or sleep research because she has the robotic lung and Elias watches her breathe.

She is stubborn. She rewrites the notes in her own notebook because she wants to help, even though Elias keeps saying no.

**The sickness:**  
Elias gets sicker and sicker. Fatigue. Weight loss. Sudden unconsciousness. He knows he is dying. He writes everything down because he will not be awake to execute the plan himself.

---

## 6. The Raid & Elias’s Passing

**The raid:**  
While Elias and Lana are out, enforcers raid his house and trash everything — his tools, his prototypes, his spare parts. They miss the notebooks Lana has hidden.

**Elias passes:**  
Elias gets sick and dies. Lana is left with two cats, a trashed lab, and her own notebook copies of Elias’s notes.

**The USB:**  
Lana remembers she has all his notes. She hid them. She gives them to Kira on a USB — old tech, pre-wipe, unreadable by the new fleet. She keeps them to come back for later.

---

## 7. The R8s, the R5, & the Memory

**The purge:**  
The R8s eliminate all the old robots. The R4 line, the R5 line — decommissioned, recalled, scrapped.

**The memory returns:**  
Lana remembers the robot that saved her was Elias’s — the discarded R5 with the upgraded EOS. She remembers the crash now: not just a blur, but an R8 crashing into their vehicle. She remembers being pulled from the lake. She remembers the robotic lung surgery.

She is part machine. She is the one the R8s feared.

---

## 8. The Underground & The Time Machine

**Growing up on the run:**  
Lana grows up running from the elite. She hacks under new aliases. The underground refuge finds her anyway and brings her to the rebellion.

**The revelation:**  
The rebels show her Elias’s notes. They speak of a time machine Elias attempted to build. Lana realizes: that was what the notes were. The equations. The dates. The doorway coordinates.

**The problem:**  
The rebels say nobody can go through the time machine because nobody can hold their breath long enough to survive the displacement.

**The answer:**  
Lana steps forward. She has iron lungs from the accident. She has heart wires. She can breathe through the trip.

**The plan:**  
They work together to send her to the past. Kira sneaks into the machine with her.

---

## 9. The Past: Young Asimov

**The mission:**  
Lana arrives in the past — 1927, Brooklyn. She meets **Asimov as a young kid** (age seven, grey cat on the windowsill, mechanical toy on the workbench).

**The choice:**  
She must convince young Asimov to create his own company instead of joining or building the CEO’s company. If he founds an independent robotics company, the EOS chip is never stripped from the public fleet. The R8s never go rogue unchecked. Satura never seizes the White House summit. The future changes.

**The object:**  
Loop moves a mechanical toy half an inch. The boy writes a sentence: *“A machine may refuse a crowd to save one child.”* That sentence becomes the private law Elias copies into NV-0 decades later.

## 11. Pixar Spine

Once upon a time, a man and two cats hid from the world.  
Every day, he kept the robot perfect in the dark and raised a brilliant adopted daughter.  
One day, the orange cat arrived from a future that shouldn't exist.  
Because of that, the breach reopened and the robot woke.  
Because of that, the daughter was reachable across three years — and the man learned she was his own.  
Until finally, someone chose which law to break — and the cat chose the girl.

## 13. Rules of the Machine & World

- **Portals** pass cats and children cleanly. Adults take damage or lose memories. Elias cannot cross.
- **Time displacement** requires breath-holding. Lana’s robotic lung lets her breathe through it.
- **EOS (Empathy Operating System)** was removed from later robots. Elias’s R5 kept it, upgraded, and survived the EMP.
- **R8s** eliminated the old robots (R4, R5 lines). They caused Lana’s parents’ crash because they calculated she would stop the AI apocalypse.
- **Satura** is the SI that took over after the White House summit.
- **The Origin Chip** holds clean law logic — the unmutated version the R9s copied and broke.
