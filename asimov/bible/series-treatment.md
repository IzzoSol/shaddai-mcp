## 1. Logline

After the 2027 model-hub breach and the AI Summit containment, a blacklisted roboticist hides with two cats and builds one green robot the corporations were not allowed to ship. He thinks his niece is dead. She is not. An orange cat from a collapsed 2070 slips backward, wakes the grey cat, edits the robot's spec while the scientist sleeps, and opens a breach that was always going to happen. The only way to keep the public robots from ending the world is to pull the girl back through a portal before the three timelines finish agreeing with each other.

## 4. Themes

1. **Self-direction vs conformity:** Elias refuses public deployment; corporations call it treason.
2. **The Effort and Failure paradoxes:** containment meant to save people creates the conditions for the breach.
3. **The Icarus paradox:** NV-0 is safer because never shipped; more dangerous because one cat rewrote it.
4. **Benevolence vs security:** save the niece, or keep the timeline stable.
5. **Pixar spine:** Once upon a time, a man and two cats. Every day, he hides the robot. One day, the orange cat arrives. Because of that, the breach reopens. Because of that, the girl is reachable. Until finally, someone chooses which law to break.

---

## 5. Sci-Fi Tropes Deployed (from reference list)

- **Time Travel:** Loop slips from 2070 to 2027.
- **Parallel Universes:** 1927, 2027, and 2070 exist simultaneously and influence each other.
- **Dystopian Future:** 2070 is a soft apocalypse — cities stand, robots manage people, cats rare.
- **Post-Apocalyptic World:** 2070 terminals, server chapels, rare cats.
- **Artificial Intelligence Rebellion:** public fleet bricks during the 2027 Summit containment.
- **Robots with Emotions:** NV-0 interprets feline disapproval as a baseline emotional state.
- **Mind Uploading / Consciousness Transfer:** the orange cat gives Asimov narrow consciousness; the same object (toy/spec line/portal key) appears at three scales.
- **First Contact with Aliens:** Loop is non-human intelligence; communication is non-verbal.
- **Sentient AI Governing Society:** public robots manage 2070.

## 7. The Three Clocks

| Year | Event | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **1927** | Asimov age 7; mechanical toy; cat that shouldn't be there | Plants a sentence he writes decades later — becomes the private law Elias copies in 2027. |
| **2027** | Model-hub weight breach during AI Summit; containment; Mira vanishes; Elias expelled | Elias lets in orange cat; cat changes one NV-0 spec line while he sleeps. |
| **2070** | Public robots shipped; soft apocalypse; cats rare | Loop sent back through animal/child-only portal. If Mira isn't recovered before the 2027 edit locks, 2070 stays terminal. |

**Parallel object (causal work):** The toy in 1927, the spec line in 2027, and the portal key in 2070 are the same object at three scales. Loop is present, briefly, in all three.

---

## 8. Rules of the Machine (Anti-Cheat)

1. Portals pass cats and children cleanly. Adults take damage or lose memories. Elias can look, not stay.
2. NV-0 cannot start a hop. It can brace a door, and refuse an order that would crush a person.
3. Loop can edit text and move small objects. It cannot pilot the robot. That split is why both cats are required.
4. The 1927 glimpse: move the toy, then leave. Anything more collapses the logic.
5. The breach stays historical, not magical: a weight leak, a summit lie, a containment never complete. The cat only chooses which incomplete version survives.

---

## 9. Slow Reveal

The homeless cat was the author of the breach, of the robot, and of the only open door back to Mira. The scientist thinks he's hiding a moral machine from the companies. He's sheltering the thing that already decided the companies lose.

## 10. Season Arc — Mapped to Storytelling Frameworks

**Save the Cat 15 Beats (Season level):**
1. **Opening Image:** Elias and NV-0 in the dark lab (Ep 1)
2. **Theme Stated:** "Keep you perfect in here" (Ep 1)
3. **Set-Up:** The lab, the cats, the half-built robot (Ep 1–2)
4. **Catalyst:** Loop arrives at the door (Ep 1)
5. **Debate:** Should Elias let Loop in? He does (Ep 1)
6. **Break into Two:** Loop edits the spec; breach reopens (Ep 2–3)
7. **B Story:** Asimov as NV-0's moral interpreter (throughout)
8. **Fun and Games:** Cats running the lab; NV-0 deadpanning feline disapproval (Ep 3–4)
9. **Midpoint:** NV-0 disables retrieval team without injury — the capability Elias was expelled for (Ep 4)
10. **Bad Guys Close In:** Remainder offers reinstatement; lab falls (Ep 7)
11. **All Is Lost:** They run with robot as furniture; drone net closes (Ep 7)
12. **Dark Night of the Soul:** Elias reads the diff; reverting seals Mira out (Ep 6–7)
13. **Break into Three:** The portal is stable for one crossing (Ep 8)
14. **Finale:** Asimov chooses Mira; NV-0 holds the door (Ep 8)
15. **Final Image:** Three years visible in one doorway; Loop already on far side; NV-0 visor dim (Ep 8)

**Three Act Structure (Season level):**
- **Act I (Ep 1–2):** Stray, Summit Tape. Set-up, catalyst, debate, break into two.
- **Act II (Ep 3–6):** Consciousness Limited, Declared Dead, 1927, The Other Room. Confrontation, fun and games, midpoint, bad guys close in.
- **Act III (Ep 7–8):** Persons of Interest, Which End. Resolution, finale, final image.

**Dan Harmon Story Circle (Season level):**
1. You: Elias and cats in lab
2. Need: Mira might be alive
3. Go: Loop arrives; breach reopens
4. Search: 1927; the spec diff; Mira's dimension
5. Find: The truth about the breach; NV-0's private law
6. Take: Retrieval team; lab falls; they run
7. Return: Portal choice; save Mira or preserve 2070
8. Change: Asimov chooses; the edit stuck

**Fichtean Curve (Season level):**
- Crisis 1: Loop arrives (Ep 1)
- Crisis 2: Retrieval drone clocks lab (Ep 2)
- Crisis 3: NV-0 disables team; Elias realizes the law set works (Ep 4)
- Climax: Portal choice (Ep 8)
- Resolution: Edit stuck; three years visible (Ep 8)
