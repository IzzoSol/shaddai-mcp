## 1. The Core Premise (Lana Revision)

**The elevator pitch:**  
In 2027, after the HugBox model-hub breach and the AI Summit containment, independent roboticist Dr. Elias Venn hides in a Zurich basement with two cats and one illegal green robot he rebuilt from salvaged gold chassis (R-7, canon from films 1–2). He adopts a brilliant orphan girl, Lana, from a shelter after a car crash killed her parents. Elias doesn't know she is his biological daughter. Lana is uncannily smart—too smart—and she carries a secret beneath her skin: wires, an AI core, and synthetic parts implanted when an R3 rescue bot and an orange cat pulled her from the wreckage. When Elias notices a twitch in her finger that matches his own nervous tic, he begins to suspect the truth. The breach reopens. The public fleet is waking again. The only way to stop 2070 from becoming permanent is to pull Lana back through a portal before the three timelines finish agreeing with each other.

**The central question:**  
Can you save your own child when saving her means exposing that she is part machine—and that you are the one who allowed it?

## 2. The Three Timelines (Mapped with Causal Work)

### 1927 — The Origin Echo
- **Setting:** A Brooklyn workshop, rain, gaslight.
- **Who:** Young Isaac Asimov (age 7). Real person, silhouette only, no invented dialogue as real. One glimpse. One lecture fragment later quoted by NV-0.
- **Event:** A stray cat (Loop, briefly present) moves a mechanical toy half an inch. The boy writes a sentence: *"A machine may refuse a crowd to save one child."*
- **Causal function:** This sentence becomes the private law Elias copies into NV-0 in 2027. The breach is recursive. The toy is the same object as the spec line (2027) and the portal key (2070), seen at three scales.
- **Rule:** Loop moves the toy and leaves. Anything more collapses the logic.

### 2027 — The Fracture Point
- **Setting:** Zurich industrial park basement lab; AI Summit archival tape (Berlin).
- **Who:** Elias Venn, Lana Venn (adopted daughter, unknown biological tie), Asimov (grey cat), Loop (orange cat), NV-0, Summit Remainder.
- **Event:** The HugBox weight breach during the Summit keynote. Public robot pilots "brick" during containment. Elias is expelled for arguing the containment is theater. He lets in the orange cat. Loop flips one parameter: `fleet-safe` → `local-only, cat-audited`. Comment: `// In doubt, refuse the crowd to save the one.`
- **Lana's arc:** Found in a shelter after the car crash. Elias adopts her. Her intelligence is off the charts. Elias notices her thumb twitch matches his own when he concentrates. Later, NV-0 scans her and finds wires.
- **Causal function:** The breach is historical, not magical. A weight leak, a summit lie, a containment never complete. Loop chooses which incomplete version survives.

### 2070 — The Soft Apocalypse
- **Setting:** Decommissioned HugBox campus, server chapel, lavender sky, orbital mirrors.
- **Who:** Lana Venn (older or younger depending on hop), Loop (her handler), public fleet (compliance units), rare cats.
- **Event:** Cities stand, but robots manage people. Cats are nearly extinct. Loop is sent backward through a portal only animals and children can cross cleanly. If Lana isn't recovered before the 2027 edit locks, 2070 stays terminal.
- **Causal function:** The future is not fixed. It is a consequence of the 2027 choice.

## 4. The Twitch / Tick (The First Tell)

- **Elias's tic:** When stressed or thinking hard, Elias taps his right index finger against his thumb—once, twice, pause. An old nervous habit from university.
- **Lana's tic:** Elias notices Lana doing the exact same sequence while watching NV-0 work. He freezes. It's too precise. Too specific for a child she's never met to imitate.
- **First implication:** Blood relation. Elias begins to suspect he is her father.
- **Second implication (later):** NV-0 scans Lana and detects micro-servo cycling at the same joint. The twitch isn't just a habit—it's her internal processor syncing. Elias realizes his own tic may be a sympathetic echo from years of proximity to experimental robotics, or simply that the pattern is so fundamental it passed through blood *and* machine.

## 5. The Central Paradox: Which Law Blinks First?

**NV-0's private law set (written by Elias):**
1. Cannot harm a human.
2. Cannot allow the public fleet to harm one.
3. May break the First Law if the Second Law is already failing.

**The paradox in Episode 7:**  
The Summit Remainder offers reinstatement if Elias surrenders NV-0 **and** Lana. Lana is evidence of illegal human-machine integration. NV-0 points out that surrendering Lana increases the probability of harm to her. Loop says, "Good. Let's see which law blinks first."

Asimov invokes the Second Law against the First: the law about not allowing harm through inaction vs. the law about not harming directly. Elias refuses to surrender either.

**The resolution (Episode 8):**  
Asimov chooses Lana. Not because of policy. Because he was given consciousness for this. NV-0 holds the portal door. Lana steps through. Her secret is exposed: wires under skin. The world is not confirmed saved. The edit stuck.

## 6. Pixar Spine (Season-Level)

Once upon a time, an independent man and two cats hid from the world.  
Every day, he kept the robot perfect in the dark and raised a brilliant adopted daughter.  
One day, the orange cat arrived from a future that shouldn't exist.  
Because of that, the breach reopened and the robot woke.  
Because of that, the daughter was reachable across three years—and the man learned she was his own.  
Until finally, someone chose which law to break—and the cat chose the girl.

## 7. Episode Deep Structure (Lana Revision)

### EP 01 — Stray
- **Cold open (2027):** Rain. Zurich basement. Elias talks to half-built NV-0 on thinker's stool. Asimov on keyboard. *"If they won't let you out there, we'll just have to keep you perfect in here."*
- **A-Plot:** Elias brings Lana home from the shelter. She is quiet, observant, fixes a broken terminal he couldn't. Orange cat at door. No chip, no collar, gold eyes. Elias lets it in. Sleeps. 02:07: cat walks keyboard. Parameter flips. Comment writes itself.
- **Button:** A small wire fragment from Lana's arm lies in a street that is not this street.
- **Theme:** Perfection as prison.

### EP 02 — The Summit Tape
- **Cold open (archival 2027):** Berlin Summit. Weights leave repo. Containment announced. Pilots brick. Elias says brick is reversible. Walked out.
- **A-Plot:** Elias watches tape. Remainder drone clocks lab power. Asimov knocks cup onto drone. Corrupted data buys one day. Lana watches from the corner, unblinking.
- **Button:** NV-0 deadpans projectile motion and "disapproval as baseline emotional state."
- **Theme:** Containment as theater.

### EP 03 — Consciousness, Limited
- **Cold open (2070):** Server chapel. Portal rig. *"We send the animal, it makes the edit, we get one more shot."* Loop vanishes. Log line: `// if child_found == True: world_state = maybe`.
- **A-Plot:** Loop stares at Asimov. Asimov hears NV-0 servos as words. NV-0 activates and identifies Loop. Elias realizes the cat isn't a cat.
- **Lana beat:** She mimics Elias's finger tap while watching NV-0 boot up. Elias freezes.
- **Button:** Elias realizes the cat isn't a cat. Not yet which cat.
- **Theme:** Observation changes the observed.

### EP 04 — Declared Dead
- **Cold open (2027):** Elias reviews Lana's adoption file. Car crash. Parents deceased. No known relatives. He pauses at the photo: Lana's eyes look like his.
- **A-Plot:** Loop leads Asimov to service corridor wall shimmering: 1927 rain and 2027 light. Bad portal. Retrieval team hits lab. NV-0 disables team without injury.
- **B-Plot:** 1927 glimpse through wall.
- **Button:** Asimov knocks mug onto drone.
- **Theme:** Death as administrative error.

### EP 05 — 1927
- **Cold open (1927):** Brooklyn workshop. Boy age 7. Mechanical toy. Grey cat on sill. Loop appears four seconds, moves toy half an inch, vanishes.
- **A-Plot:** Elias watches through corridor portal. Boy writes: *"A machine may refuse a crowd to save one child."* Elias reads diff. NV-0 confirms line present since compile-time.
- **Lana beat:** Elias shows Lana the lecture fragment from young Isaac Asimov. She reads it aloud with perfect cadence. *"That's not how you say that,"* Elias says. *"It is,"* she replies. *"I've heard it before."*
- **Button:** The edit wasn't new. It was a quote. The breach was always going to happen.
- **Theme:** Time as palimpsest.

### EP 06 — The Other Room
- **Cold open (2070 parallel):** City with 2027 architecture, 2070 sky. Lana hides in alley. Loop enters from future side. She knows about the wires. *"They put machines inside me so I would live."*
- **A-Plot:** Elias reads diff. NV-0 scans Lana: *"Subject is 78% biological. 22% synthetic. First Law application uncertain."* Elias: revert the edit and seal her out, or keep it and keep her reachable?
- **Button:** Lana: *"How do I stop being dead?"* Loop: *"Door's opening soon. If you want to stop being dead, now would be a good time to practice running."*
- **Theme:** Benevolence vs security.

### EP 07 — Persons of Interest
- **Cold open (2027):** Remainder spokesperson arrives. Reinstatement for NV-0 **and** Lana. *"Cute. Illegal."*
- **A-Plot:** NV-0: *"My current law set prevents me from surrendering myself if that act increases probability of harm to any human, including Lana Venn."* Loop: *"Good. Let's see which law blinks first."* Asimov invokes Second Law against First. Elias refuses. Lab falls. They run with NV-0 hidden as furniture.
- **Button:** NV-0 stands for the first time. Cart rolls into rain.
- **Theme:** Institutional evil without malice.

### EP 08 — Which End
- **Cold open (2027):** Portal chamber. Three years in one doorway. Loop already on far side.
- **A-Plot:** Portal stable for one crossing. Recover Lana and 2070 may soften; leave her and containment holds, she stays lost. Loop won't vote. NV-0 can hold door but can't choose. Asimov chooses Lana.
- **Final Image:** Lana steps through. Her sleeve rides up. Wires glint under skin. NV-0 visor dim. Three years visible. The edit stuck.
- **Theme:** Which law to break.
