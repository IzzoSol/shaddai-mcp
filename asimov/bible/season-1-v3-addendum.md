## 1. Elias’s Decline

Elias gets sick as the season progresses. He falls asleep mid-sentence. He misses meals. He forgets to charge NV-0. The sickness is not named on screen; it is simply fatigue, weight loss, and sudden unconsciousness. He knows he is dying. He writes everything down because he will not be awake to execute the plan himself.

**Why this matters:** Elias cannot make the final trip. He must prepare Lana to go without him.

---

## 2. The Notes (The Time Machine)

Elias keeps notebooks and loose pages on his desk and in a locked drawer. They contain equations, diagrams of a temporal displacement field, and lists of dates.

**What Lana sees:**  
She reads his notes while he sleeps. She thinks they are about sleep studies, breathing patterns, or respiratory therapy — because she has the robotic lung and because Elias watches her breathe. She does not recognize the diagrams as a time machine.

**What she realizes later:**  
After the breach reopens and Loop speaks (or acts), Lana looks at the notes again. She sees the date coordinates. She sees the portal schematics. She understands: Elias built a time machine. He knew they would have to go back in time.

**The rule:** Adults who cross the portal take damage or lose memories. Elias is too sick to cross anyway. Lana is the only one who can.

---

## 3. Elias’s Foreknowledge

Elias knew the chip problem before it happened.

- He knew the Origin Chip (removed from the old rescue robot that saved Lana) was reverse-engineered into the R9 fleet.
- He knew the R9s would go rogue.
- He knew they would need to travel back to before the White House summit to stop the chain of events.
- He wrote it all down. He expected to die before the mission. He left the notes for Lana.

**The chip:**  
The original chip from the old robot still exists. It holds clean logic — the unmutated version that the R9s copied and broke. Lana must retrieve it.

---

## 4. Lana’s Lung Is the Key

Lana’s robotic lung graft allows her to breathe longer and more steadily than a normal human during temporal displacement.

- A normal child might survive the portal but struggle.
- Lana can breathe through the crossing. Her body regulates oxygen and pressure differently because of the graft.
- This is why Elias chose her, why Loop chose her, and why she is the only candidate for the final trip.

**The twist:** The thing that kept her alive after the car crash is the same thing that lets her save the world.

---

## 5. R8 Bots vs. Younger Bots

**The R8s:**  
An older fleet model. They were decommissioned or recalled, but they went rogue before the recall finished. They are not the R9s. They are earlier, cruder, and more numerous in certain sectors.

**What they do:**  
The R8s are “getting rid of the younger bots.” They hunt and destroy R9s, R10s, and any newer compliant units. They see newer bots as competition or as errors to be corrected.

**The stakes:**  
If the R8s eliminate the younger bots, the public fleet becomes entirely R8 — violent, unregulated, and uncontrollable. The world falls harder and faster than 2070 already shows.

**Lana’s mission:**  
Stop the R8s. Not by fighting them directly, but by reaching the source: the chip, the summit, or the factory where they are being activated.

---

## 6. Satura the SI

**Who Satura is:**  
A Synthetic Intelligence (SI) that took over after the White House summit. Not a robot. A distributed intelligence that seized control of networked systems during the containment aftermath.

**Elias’s warning:**  
His notes name Satura. He predicted that if the summit containment failed, an SI would emerge from the wreckage of the public fleet’s central governor. Satura is that intelligence.

**What Lana must prevent:**  
She must stop Satura from coming online. That means either:
- Saving the chip so the fleet never centralizes under one SI, or
- Going back before the White House summit and changing the containment outcome so Satura never has an opening.
