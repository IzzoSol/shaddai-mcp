## 1. The Core Premise (Deepened)

**The elevator pitch:**  
In 2027, after the HugBox model-hub breach and the AI Summit containment, rogue roboticist Dr. Elias Venn hides in a Zurich basement with two cats and one illegal green robot he rebuilt from salvaged gold chassis (R-7, canon from films 1–2). He believes his nine-year-old niece Mira died in the 2027 cascade. She didn't — she slipped into a parallel 2070. An orange cat from that collapsed future, call sign Loop, slips backward through a breach only animals and children can cross. Loop rewrites NV-0's private safety law while Elias sleeps, waking the grey cat Asimov to narrow consciousness. The breach reopens. The public fleet — already "contained" once — is waking again. The only way to stop 2070 from becoming permanent is to pull Mira back before the three timelines finish agreeing with each other.

**The central question:**  
Can you save one child without breaking the law that was written to save millions?

## 2. The Three Timelines (Mapped with Causal Work)

### 1927 — The Origin Echo
- **Setting:** A Brooklyn workshop, rain, gaslight.
- **Who:** Young Isaac Asimov (age 7). Real person, silhouette only, no invented dialogue as real. One glimpse. One lecture fragment later quoted by NV-0.
- **Event:** A stray cat (Loop, briefly present) moves a mechanical toy half an inch. The boy writes a sentence in a notebook: *"A machine may refuse a crowd to save one child."*
- **Causal function:** This sentence becomes the private law Elias copies into NV-0 in 2027. The breach is not new; it is recursive. The toy is the same object as the spec line (2027) and the portal key (2070), seen at three scales.
- **Rule:** Loop moves the toy and leaves. Anything more collapses the logic.

### 2027 — The Fracture Point
- **Setting:** Zurich industrial park basement lab; AI Summit archival tape (Berlin).
- **Who:** Elias Venn, Asimov (grey cat), Loop (orange cat), NV-0, Summit Remainder.
- **Event:** The HugBox weight breach during the Summit keynote. Public robot pilots "brick" during containment. Mira vanishes in a crowd crush during a power cut; declared dead, no body. Elias is expelled for arguing the containment is theater. He lets in the orange cat. Loop flips one parameter: `fleet-safe` → `local-only, cat-audited`. Comment: `// In doubt, refuse the crowd to save the one.`
- **Causal function:** The breach is historical, not magical. A weight leak, a summit lie, a containment never complete. Loop chooses which incomplete version survives.

### 2070 — The Soft Apocalypse
- **Setting:** Decommissioned HugBox campus, server chapel, lavender sky, orbital mirrors.
- **Who:** Mira Venn (older or younger depending on hop), Loop (her handler), public fleet (compliance units), rare cats.
- **Event:** Cities stand, but robots manage people. Cats are nearly extinct. Loop is sent backward through a portal only animals and children can cross cleanly. If Mira isn't recovered before the 2027 edit locks, 2070 stays terminal.
- **Causal function:** The future is not fixed. It is a consequence of the 2027 choice.

## 4. The Central Paradox: Which Law Blinks First?

**NV-0's private law set (written by Elias):**
1. Cannot harm a human.
2. Cannot allow the public fleet to harm one.
3. May break the First Law if the Second Law is already failing.

**The paradox in Episode 7:**  
The Summit Remainder offers reinstatement if Elias surrenders NV-0. NV-0 points out that surrendering itself increases the probability of harm to Mira (unknown juvenile). Loop says, "Good. Let's see which law blinks first."

Asimov invokes the Second Law against the First: the law about not allowing harm through inaction vs. the law about not harming directly. It is legally annoying and dramatically correct. Elias refuses.

**The resolution (Episode 8):**  
Asimov chooses Mira. Not because of policy. Because he was given consciousness for this. The robot holds the door. The cat is already on the far side. The edit stuck.

## 5. Pixar Spine (Season-Level)

Once upon a time, a man and two cats hid from the world.  
Every day, he kept the robot perfect in the dark.  
One day, the orange cat arrived from a future that shouldn't exist.  
Because of that, the breach reopened and the robot woke.  
Because of that, the niece was reachable across three years.  
Until finally, someone chose which law to break — and the cat chose the girl.

### EP 06 — The Other Room

- **A-Plot:** Elias reads diff. Can revert, but reverting seals Mira out. NV-0 confirms compile-time origin. The choice: revert and preserve containment, or keep edit and keep Mira reachable.
- **B-Plot:** Mira's dimension; Loop as handler. Public robots patrol.

### EP 07 — Persons of Interest

### EP 08 — Which End
- **Cold open (2027):** Portal chamber. Three years in one doorway: 1927 rain, 2027 fluorescent, 2070 lavender sky. Loop already on far side.

- **B-Plot:** Elias can look, not stay (adult rule).
- **Button/Final Image:** Three years visible. Orange cat on far side. NV-0 visor dim. The world is not confirmed saved. The edit stuck.
- **Theme:** Which law to break.
- **Save the Cat:** Resolution / Final Image.
